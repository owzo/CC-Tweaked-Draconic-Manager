# Code of conduct

This project is a small, volunteer-run tool for a game. We want everyone
who uses it, reports a problem or sends a change to be treated with
respect, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be patient and kind, especially with newcomers to ComputerCraft, Lua or
  the reactor mods.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming a report or change is wrong.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and accept it the same way.
- Credit other people's work, including other projects this one builds on.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spamming.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  shared, professional space.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
the wiki, and commit messages. It also applies when someone represents the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@owzo](https://github.com/owzo):

- privately, through GitHub's
  [private reporting form](https://github.com/owzo/CC-Tweaked-Draconic-Manager/security/advisories/new)
  (it accepts any confidential report, not only security issues);
- or by asking in an issue for a private contact. Don't include the
  details in the issue.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone involved, in
proportion to what happened.
